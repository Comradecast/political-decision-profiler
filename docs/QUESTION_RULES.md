# Question Design Rules (V1.1)

## Core Principle

Every question must force a meaningful tradeoff.

If a question can be answered without cost, tension, or consequence, it is invalid.

---

## Required Components

Each scenario must include:

* A potential benefit
* A real cost or downside
* Either:

  * Uncertainty (Intervention Style), OR
  * Guaranteed consequence (Risk Tolerance)

---

## Valid Question Template

A situation presents [benefit], but involves [cost] and [uncertainty or consequence].

Which approach do you choose?

---

## Dimension-Specific Rules

### Risk Tolerance

* Must assume outcome certainty
* Must NOT include:

  * uncertainty
  * prevention
  * intervention timing

---

### Intervention Style

* Must include uncertainty or incomplete evidence
* Must focus on timing of action
* Must NOT assume known outcomes

---

### Time Horizon

* Must include:

  * present cost
  * delayed benefit
  * uncertainty of success
* Must avoid moral framing (“responsible”, “better”)

---

### System Trust

* Must be framed as belief:

  * “Do you believe…”
* Must target specific domain:

  * government or corporate

---

## Invalid Question Types (Reject Automatically)

* No tradeoff
* Obvious “correct” answer
* Multiple dimensions mixed without control
* Moral or virtue-signaling framing
* Vague or abstract wording

---

## Validation Requirement

All questions must pass validation before entering the system.

Invalid questions are rejected, not revised.
